Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026

Toddler Read is designed to work without accounts, ads, analytics, cloud sync, or Toddler Read servers. Most activity stays on your device. Network access is used only for features that need content or services outside the app, such as playing a QR code that points to online audio or using an optional third-party provider in the card generator.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to Toddler Read, including the Toddler Reader Android app, the browser/PWA reader, and the Toddler QR Card Generator published at https://toddler-read.netlify.app/.

Information we collect

Toddler Read does not collect personal information on developer-operated servers. The app does not provide user accounts, advertising, analytics, cloud synchronization, or remote storage controlled by Toddler Read.

Camera access

The reader asks for camera access so it can scan QR codes. Camera frames are processed on the device for QR detection. They are not sent to Toddler Read servers.

QR code content and playback

QR codes may contain text, embedded audio data, local file paths, or online audio/media URLs. Text QR codes are spoken using the device or browser text-to-speech engine. Embedded audio is played locally by the app.

If a QR code points to an online audio file or media service, your device contacts that third-party host directly to load or play the content. Those third parties may receive normal technical information such as your IP address, device/browser information, and the requested URL according to their own privacy practices. Toddler Read does not receive that traffic.

Generator data

The card generator stores card data, images, settings, language choices, and provider configuration in your browser's local storage or IndexedDB. This data stays in that browser unless you export it, clear browser data, or use an optional provider feature described below.

Optional third-party provider features

The generator can optionally make direct requests from your browser to third-party providers for translation, image search, or image generation. These features run only when you configure or use them. API keys you enter are stored locally in your browser and are sent directly to the selected provider when needed. Toddler Read does not proxy or store those requests.

Sharing

Toddler Read does not sell personal data and does not share personal data with Toddler Read servers. Data may be sent to third-party services only when you use a feature that directly contacts them, such as opening a QR code with a hosted audio/media URL or using an optional generator provider.

Retention and deletion

Because Toddler Read does not receive or store your app data on developer-operated servers, there is no server-side account or cloud data for us to delete. Local generator data can be deleted by clearing the browser's site data for Toddler Read. Android app data can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

Toddler Read is intended to be set up by a parent, guardian, caregiver, or educator for use by young children. The app is designed to avoid accounts, advertising, analytics, and developer-operated collection of children's personal information.

Security

Toddler Read minimizes data handling by keeping data local where possible and by sending optional provider requests directly from your device to the provider you choose. Public web versions are served over HTTPS.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the project's public support page: https://ko-fi.com/emedware.
